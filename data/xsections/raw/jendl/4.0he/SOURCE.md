# JENDL-4.0/HE residual-production tables

- Index: https://wwwndc.jaea.go.jp/ftpnd/jendl/jendl40he_prod.html
- Retrieval date: 2026-07-24
- Targets: C-12, N-14 and O-16 with incident protons

The tables are the JAEA precomputed residual-production representation. The
upstream description states that they combine the MF=3, MT=5 reaction cross
section with the MF=6, MT=5 relative production of each residual nucleus.
