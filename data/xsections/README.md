# Proton-production cross sections

This directory stores the public cross-section inputs used to quantify the
positron-emitter production uncertainty. It has three data layers:

- `raw/`: immutable files as distributed by EXFOR, TENDL and JENDL;
- `normalized/`: one-to-one conversions into the common point schema;
- `curation.csv` and `point_curation.csv`: dataset- and point-level EXFOR
  admissibility audits;
- `reaction_thresholds.csv`: laboratory thresholds calculated from AME2020
  atomic masses for the two corrected oxygen channels;
- `fits/`: nominal EXFOR fits, correlated uncertainty replicas, and the named
  B0095 sensitivity fit;
- `models/`: documented interpolation-ready curves used for comparisons and
  transport diagnostics.

Covariance tables derived from an evaluated source live in `covariance/`.
`sources.csv` identifies every upstream distribution or file, its retrieval
location, revision and checksum. `SCHEMA.md` defines the normalized and model
tables.

## Current raw snapshot

- EXFOR Plot File commit `57052199247e2f64862a320e3930d174318f5e6c`, dated
  2026-06-29: 85 CX4 experimental series.
  - 30 series for C-12(p,x)C-11;
  - 12 series for O-16(p,x)C-11;
  - 12 series for O-16(p,x)O-15;
  - 13 series for N-14(p,x)N-13.
  - 18 inclusive or exclusive series for O-16 to N-13.
- The IAEA recommended O-16(p,alpha)N-13 excitation function and tabulated
  uncertainty.
- Incident-proton files served by the TENDL-2023 distribution for C-12, N-14
  and O-16. Their internal headers identify the adopted LANL ENDF/B-VII.1
  evaluations dated 1996--1997.
- JENDL-4.0/HE residual-production tables for proton interactions on C-12,
  N-14 and O-16.

The Geant4 residual-production curves are generated locally and retained as
model diagnostics. Selected-channel production is calculated by folding the
EXFOR curves with the target-resolved proton exposure from transport.

The historical direct QGSP_BIC_HP denominator pilot is generated by
`tools/build_g4_denominator.py`. It queries the total inelastic cross section
and samples 10,000 final states per pure-target energy point. Its support audit
is stored beside the pilot curves under the versioned Geant4 directory. These
curves diagnose the residual final-state model and are not inputs to the
production estimator.

## Current EXFOR curation

The current policy accepts 79 of the 85 series. Six remain pending:

- B0077.002 and B0077.003 carry the SF8 `A` modifier, which denotes a cross
  section multiplied by isotopic abundance;
- B0095.002 contains two near-threshold points whose 1 MeV incident-energy
  field has not yet been classified from the source publication; and
- Masuda E2568.002, E2568.003 and E2568.004 are relative excitation functions
  sharing an external normalization.

Pending series do not enter the nominal fit or its campaign-dispersion
estimate. Masuda is displayed at its published scale as a shape comparison.
B0095 is also included in a named central-value sensitivity fit.
