\begin{abstract}
Experimental EXFOR data define the nominal excitation functions and 1000
correlated replicas for five positron-emitter production channels.  This note
specifies how these curves are combined with one frozen Geant4 proton
transport.  Target-resolved proton track length is folded directly with each
external cross section to calculate emitter production.  Geant4 determines
the charged-particle transport, energy loss, total inelastic attenuation and
dose; the fitted experimental curves determine production through the selected
channels.  The same transport realization is used for the nominal curve and
all replicas.  Complete transports with BIC, INCL++ and Bertini form a separate
hadronic-transport envelope.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objective and frozen scope}

The first implementation covers
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
  &^{12}\mathrm{C}(p,x)^{11}\mathrm{C},\qquad
   ^{16}\mathrm{O}(p,x)^{15}\mathrm{O},\qquad
   ^{16}\mathrm{O}(p,x)^{11}\mathrm{C},\\
  &^{14}\mathrm{N}(p,x)^{13}\mathrm{N},\qquad
   ^{16}\mathrm{O}(p,x)^{13}\mathrm{N}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:channels}
\end{equation}
The nominal cross sections and their replica ensembles are documented in
\file{docs/xsection\_fit.tex}.  JENDL-4.0/HE and LANL ENDF/B-VII.1 remain
comparisons with the fitted experimental curves.  The distal-edge calculation
uses the EXFOR fit and replicas.

Two quantities are reported for replica $r$:
\begin{align}
  b_{\mathrm{prod}}^{(r)} & =
  R_{50,\mathrm{prod}}^{(r)}-R_{50,\mathrm{prod}}^{(50)},
  \label{eq:bprod}\\
  b_{\mathrm{reco}}^{(r)} & =
  R_{50,\mathrm{reco}}^{(r)}-R_{50,\mathrm{reco}}^{(50)}.
  \label{eq:breco}
\end{align}
Here the superscript 50 denotes the pointwise replica-median cross section.
The first displacement is available from the production profile in this
repository.  The second follows the common acquisition, detector simulation,
reconstruction and edge estimator.

The calculation separates two sources of variation:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item the \emph{experimental cross-section uncertainty}, obtained by folding
        every replica with the same frozen proton transport; and
  \item the \emph{hadronic-transport envelope}, obtained by repeating the
        transport with BIC, INCL++ and Bertini and folding the same external
        cross-section curves with each resulting track-length field.
\end{enumerate}
The two results are reported separately.  The first measures uncertainty in
the selected production cross sections at fixed transport.  The second
measures the dependence of proton and secondary-proton fluence on the Geant4
cascade model.

\section{Calculation architecture}

The calculation is organized as
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.91\textwidth}
Geant4 proton transport
$\longrightarrow$ target-resolved proton step history
$\longrightarrow$ track-length folding with the EXFOR curves
$\longrightarrow$ isotope production budgets and spatial profiles
$\longrightarrow$ positron transport and annihilation source
$\longrightarrow$ beam-off handoff and reconstructed $R_{50}$.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}

The Geant4 transport is run once for all nominal and replica calculations.  It
records steps from primary and secondary protons in the phantom.  The required
step information is
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item event and proton-track identifiers;
  \item pre- and post-step position and step length;
  \item pre- and post-step proton kinetic energy;
  \item material or the number densities of $^{12}$C, $^{14}$N and $^{16}$O;
  \item proton statistical weight.
\end{itemize}
A representative step energy is obtained from the pre- and post-step energies.
The adopted midpoint or path-averaged rule will be tested by subdividing the
steps.

Two tracked transport products serve different stages of the calculation.
First, a binned track-length kernel stores the total proton path length for
each target isotope, proton-energy bin and spatial bin.  This compact object is
sufficient for isotope budgets, production profiles and
$R_{50,\mathrm{prod}}$.  Second, a fixed proposal sample of step segments
retains their positions and sampling probabilities.  It supplies production
sites to the positron-transport stage without storing the complete raw step
history for every replica.

\section{Direct folding of external cross sections}

Let $s$ be a proton step with length $\ell_s$, representative energy $E_s$,
and statistical weight $a_s$.  Let $n_{t,s}$ be the number density of target
isotope $t$ in the material traversed by that step.  The expected number of
residual nuclei produced through channel $c$ for replica $r$ is
\begin{equation}
  \Delta N_{s,c}^{(r)}=
  a_s n_{t(c),s}\ell_s\sigma_c^{(r)}(E_s).
  \label{eq:step-yield}
\end{equation}
The implementation converts millibarns to area before evaluating this
expression.  The production of isotope $j$ in spatial bin $m$ is
\begin{equation}
  P_{j,m}^{(r)}=
  \sum_{s\in m}\sum_{c\rightarrow j}
  a_s n_{t(c),s}\ell_s\sigma_c^{(r)}(E_s).
  \label{eq:profile}
\end{equation}
The nominal calculation substitutes the replica median
$\sigma_c^{(50)}(E)$.  Every replica substitutes one complete, correlated
curve.  This construction preserves correlations across energy and between
all spatial regions sampled by the same proton field.

For the five channels in Eq.~\eqref{eq:channels}, Eqs.~\eqref{eq:step-yield}
and \eqref{eq:profile} define the complete production tally.  Residual nuclei
created for these channels by the Geant4 final-state model are omitted from
that tally.  Geant4 remains responsible for proton transport, secondary-proton
transport, continuous energy loss, multiple scattering, nuclear attenuation,
dose and positron transport.  This division assigns selected-channel
production to the experimentally fitted cross sections and avoids counting the
same production twice.

The method holds the proton fluence fixed while a selected partial production
cross section varies.  The relevant partial cross sections are tens of
millibarns, compared with a total proton inelastic cross section of several
hundred millibarns.  A tracked diagnostic will evaluate
\begin{equation}
  f(E)=\frac{\sum_c\sigma_c(E)}{\sigma_{\mathrm{inel}}^{(\mathrm{G4})}(E)}
  \label{eq:fluence-check}
\end{equation}
over the energies and materials sampled by the treatment field.  This ratio
sets the scale of the first-order attenuation correction associated with the
fixed-transport approximation.

\section{Production sites and positron transport}

Equation~\eqref{eq:profile} yields expected production rather than an integer
list of residual nuclei.  A common proposal sample converts the expectation
into a spatial source.  A step segment $s$ and channel $c$ are sampled from a
fixed proposal density $q_{s,c}$ that covers every selected target, energy and
position.  For replica $r$, its source contribution is proportional to
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\Delta N_{s,c}^{(r)}}{q_{s,c}}.
  \label{eq:proposal}
\end{equation}
The nominal and all replicas use the same proposal sites and random-number
streams.  The resulting pairing reduces Monte Carlo noise in the displacement
between two edge estimates.

The selected residual isotope is created at the sampled production site and
its positron is transported through the local Geant4 material.  The output
contains both the production and annihilation coordinates.  The existing
beam-off handoff then applies isotope half-lives and acquisition timing to the
production budgets.  Production-profile and reconstructed-edge displacements
remain distinct outputs throughout the analysis.

throughout the measured domain and uses the same experimental curve for its
nominal prediction and variations.  The pilot data and their tracked generator
are retained for model diagnosis and implementation validation.

\section{Hadronic-transport envelope}

The transport calculation will accept a Geant4 reference physics list at run
time.  The first set is
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \file{QGSP\_BIC\_HP};
  \item \file{QGSP\_INCLXX\_HP};
  \item \file{QGSP\_BERT\_HP}.
\end{itemize}
Each physics-list name is recorded in the run metadata and run tag.  Each run
produces an independent target-resolved track-length kernel.  The same nominal
EXFOR curves are folded with the three kernels.  The signed changes in
$b_{\mathrm{prod}}$ and $b_{\mathrm{reco}}$ are reported individually, and
the largest absolute change relative to BIC defines the initial envelope.
This envelope probes changes in primary and secondary proton transport.  The
external production cross sections remain common to all three calculations.

\section{Uncertainty estimator}

All 1000 cross-section replicas can be evaluated on one stored track-length
kernel without repeating proton transport.  The folding consists of array
operations over channel, energy and spatial bins.  The computationally
expensive positron and detector stages use a fixed proposal sample.  If the
downstream sample size makes propagation of all replicas impractical, a
stratified subset is selected across quantiles of the production-edge shift
and isotope-yield changes; the full ensemble still defines the production
uncertainty.

For either edge observable, let $q_{16}$, $q_{50}$ and $q_{84}$ be the
percentiles of the paired replica displacement.  The reported central bias is
$q_{50}$, the asymmetric interval is $(q_{50}-q_{16},q_{84}-q_{50})$, and the
single-number cross-section uncertainty is
\begin{equation}
  u_{\mathrm{xs}}=\frac{q_{84}-q_{16}}{2}.
  \label{eq:uncertainty}
\end{equation}
The full displacement distribution and isotope-resolved production changes are
retained with the scalar summary.

\section{Validation requirements}

The implementation is accepted when the following checks pass:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A uniform-slab calculation reproduces the analytic expectation
        $N=n\ell\sigma$ for a constant input cross section.
  \item Tabulated cross sections are reproduced at their grid points, remain
        zero at and below the configured thresholds, and follow the documented
        interpolation rule between points.
  \item Subdivision of proton steps and refinement of the energy and spatial
        kernel bins leave the production budgets and edge positions stable at
        the adopted tolerance.
  \item A direct per-step sum agrees with the binned track-length calculation.
  \item Substituting the nominal and a selected replica reproduces their known
        production-ratio change in a mono-energetic slab.
  \item The selected-channel source contains only the externally folded tally,
        and a dedicated counter verifies the residual-nucleus exclusion.
  \item Dose and the stored proton track-length field are identical for every
        replica evaluated on a frozen transport.
  \item Fixed seeds reproduce the proposal sites, isotope budgets and paired
        edge shifts.
  \item Positron-range distributions from the externally sampled sources agree
        with the corresponding isotope and material reference calculations.
  \item Alternative physics lists write distinct run directories and record
        the selected list in their metadata.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Implementation sequence}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Add target-resolved proton step scoring and define the tracked schema
        for raw step segments, binned kernels and run metadata.
  \item Implement cross-section interpolation and Eq.~\eqref{eq:step-yield},
        followed by the analytic slab, interpolation and bin-refinement tests.
  \item Generate a moderate-statistics nominal SOBP transport and construct its
        target--energy--position track-length kernel.
  \item Fold the median curves and all 1000 replicas, then write isotope
        budgets, production profiles, $R_{50,\mathrm{prod}}$ values and the
        paired uncertainty distribution.
  \item Implement the common spatial proposal and Geant4 positron-source pass,
        preserving both production and annihilation coordinates.
  \item Apply the existing beam-off handoff and propagate a stratified replica
        set through the downstream detector and reconstruction calculation.
  \item Repeat the proton transport with INCL++ and Bertini, fold the common
        external curves and report the independent transport envelope.
  \item Generate the final high-statistics source after the closure tests and
        pilot convergence criteria have been satisfied.
\end{enumerate}

All calculations, tables and figures are produced by tracked repository code.
The generated data record the input fit version, transport run identifier,
physics list, Geant4 version, random seed, binning and software revision.
